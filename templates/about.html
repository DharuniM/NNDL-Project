About SemanticReads

Academic research context, problem motivation, and architectural solution
THE PROBLEM

Challenges in Large-Scale Digital Library Discovery

Modern digital libraries host millions of titles, yet readers frequently struggle to find relevant literature suited to their specific thematic interests and individual taste profiles. Conventional recommendation systems suffer from severe structural limitations:

Information Overload Too many unstructured options overwhelm readers without intelligent guidance.
Keyword-Based Search Exact lexical matching fails to uncover conceptual and thematic parallels.
Popularity Bias Algorithms repeatedly recommend the same blockbusters, neglecting the long-tail catalog.
Cold-Start & Interaction Sparsity New items and users lack sufficient interaction matrices in standard collaborative filtering.
PROPOSED SOLUTION

Dual-Branch Neural Hybrid Recommendation Framework

SemanticReads unifies dense contextual embeddings with non-linear neural collaborative networks:

  • Semantic Branch: Sentence-BERT (all-MiniLM-L6-v2) captures latent conceptual relationships across metadata, indexing high-dimensional vectors via FAISS for sub-millisecond retrieval.
  • Collaborative Branch: A deep Multi-Layer Perceptron (NCF) maps user-item interaction histories into non-linear preference predictions.
  • Hybrid Ranking: A dynamically weighted score fusion (0.60 × NCF + 0.40 × Semantic) eliminates single-modality bias while offering transparent "Why this book?" explanations.
