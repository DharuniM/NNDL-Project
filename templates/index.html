How Our Recommendation Engine Works

Dual-branch hybrid architecture fusing semantic representations with neural collaborative filtering
1

Understand Books

Sentence-BERT (all-MiniLM-L6-v2) converts book titles, authors, and publishers into 384-dimensional dense semantic vector representations.

2

Learn Reader Preferences

Neural Collaborative Filtering (NCF) passes user and book latent embeddings through deep Multi-Layer Perceptrons (Dense 64-32-16) to capture non-linear affinities.

3

Retrieve Similar Books

FAISS (IndexFlatIP) performs sub-millisecond vector similarity search in normalized Euclidean space to retrieve semantically related candidate books.

4

Hybrid Ranking

Collaborative affinity (60%) and semantic similarity (40%) are normalized and combined into a final unified hybrid score for personalized Top-N delivery.
