Technical Component Specifications

Deep dive into the neural and algorithmic models powering the platform

1. Sentence-BERT / MiniLM

We employ the pre-trained all-MiniLM-L6-v2 Sentence-Transformer model to project structured book metadata (Title, Author, and Publisher) into a continuous 384-dimensional semantic manifold.

  • Contextual self-attention captures cross-attribute thematic relationships.
  • All output vectors are unit-normalized ($L_2 = 1.0$), ensuring inner product queries equal cosine similarity.
  • Eliminates vocabulary mismatch and enables nuanced content matching.

2. Neural Collaborative Filtering

Traditional matrix factorization models user-item interactions through linear dot products. NCF replaces this with a deep Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) network to learn high-order non-linear collaborative patterns.

  • Learns 32-dimensional latent representations for users and books.
  • Concatenated representations feed through Dense layers (64 → 32 → 16) with ReLU activations and Dropout regularization.
  • Sigmoid output maps affinity directly into a normalized preference scale $[0, 1]$.

3. FAISS Vector Retrieval

Facebook AI Similarity Search (FAISS) enables high-throughput nearest neighbor lookup across high-dimensional vector spaces.

  • Utilizes IndexFlatIP (Inner Product on normalized vectors).
  • Sub-millisecond retrieval of candidate nearest neighbors without exhaustive search overhead.
  • Pre-built index is loaded directly into RAM at Flask startup.

4. Hybrid Score Fusion

Solves the cold-start and sparsity limitations of pure collaborative filtering and the narrow diversity of pure content filtering through weighted linear fusion:

Score = 0.60 × NCF_Score + 0.40 × Semantic_Score
  • Both sub-scores are min-max normalized prior to weighted combination.
  • Weights are centrally configurable via config.py.
  • Already-read books are dynamically pruned before delivering the final Top-N ranking.
